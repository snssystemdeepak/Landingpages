Added Lincoln Drive Modes

Two additional drive modes in the Lincoln Grand Touring model let you choose how best to use your vehicle's electric power. Select the Preserve EV drive mode for full-hybrid performance using both engine and electric motor or Pure EV for electric-only driving. Both modes provide a smooth driving experience on city streets and highways.

Grand Touring tips

Lincoln Co-Pilot360® 2.1 TECHOLOGY

Adaptive Cruise Control 2.0

Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC) 2.0 lets you set a cruising speed and distance from the vehicle ahead of you. When the vehicle ahead slows down, so do you. When traffic picks back up, you resume your preset speed and distance. With the Stop-and-Go function, the ACC system can bring your vehicle to a complete stop in congested traffic and resume driving as traffic begins to move.* Lane centering, Speed Sign Recognition, and Predictive Speed Assist add more confidence behind the wheel.

Lane-Keeping System

The Lane-Keeping System with Blind Spot Assist uses camera technology to monitor road lane markings and detect unintentional drifting toward the outside of a lane. If the camera detects an impending unintentional drift, the system will use the steering system and the instrument cluster display to alert and/or aid you to stay in your lane.*

Blind Spot Information System with Cross-Traffic Alert

The Blind Spot Information System can identify and alert you to vehicles entering your blind spots. Cross-Traffic Alert can detect traffic approaching from either side of you while reversing.*

Pre-Collision Assist® with Automatic Emergency Braking

Pre-Collision® Assist with Automatic Emergency Braking (AEB) scans the road ahead and offers audible and visual warnings if there are obstacles or pedestrians detected in your path. If an impact becomes imminent, and the driver doesn't take corrective action, the brakes can automatically apply by themselves to lessen the severity of traffic accidents.*

Automatic high beams

When driving with your high beams on, the automatic high beam system in the Lincoln Corsair® SUV can detect approaching light and can turn your high beams on and off as needed to help give you greater visibility.*

Available Lincoln Co-Pilot360® 2.1 Vision

Expand your sense of awareness. Adding to Lincoln Co-Pilot360® 2.1, Lincoln Co-Pilot360® 2.1 Vision includes a 360-Degree Camera with side-mounted cameras to provide a clear view of what’s around you.* Additional front and rear parking sensors and Reverse Brake Assist help ensure you are in the clear.
