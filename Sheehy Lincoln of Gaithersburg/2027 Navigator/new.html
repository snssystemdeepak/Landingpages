Q&A About the 2027 Lincoln Navigator

1. What engine comes standard in the 2027 Lincoln Navigator Rockville, MD?

Every trim level is equipped with a robust Twin-Turbocharged 3.5L V6 engine that generates an impressive 440 horsepower and 510 lb.-ft. of torque.

2. What size are the digital screens inside the cabin?

This luxury SUV features a standard 11.1-inch LCD center touchscreen display paired with a massive, dash-spanning 48-inch panoramic display that offers customizable themes and widgets.

3. How many passengers can fit in the 2027 Lincoln Navigator interior?

The 2027 Lincoln Navigator provides a flexible and spacious three-row interior layout designed to comfortably accommodate seven to eight people.

4. What are the available audio system options?

This luxury SUV comes with a standard Revel® Audio System featuring HD Radio™ Technology and 14 speakers, while true audiophiles can upgrade to a premium 28-speaker Revel® Ultima 3D Audio System for concert-hall sound quality.

Yes, advanced safety technology comes standard on every configuration, including Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop-and-Go, Lane Centering Assist, and Predictive Speed Assist.
