Meet the 2026 Ford Bronco® Plymouth, MA, at Colonial Ford

The call of the great outdoors requires a machine built specifically to conquer it. The 2026 Ford Bronco® Plymouth, MA, redefines off-road exploration by combining legendary ruggedness with cutting-edge modern engineering. Our team at Colonial Ford is eager to introduce this unstoppable 4x4 SUV to adventurers across New England.

Which Ford dealers have the 2026 Ford Bronco® in Plymouth, MA, in stock?

Visit our Ford dealership to inspect the impressive powertrains driving this trailblazer. The 2026 Ford Bronco® Plymouth, MA, starts strong with a 2.3L EcoBoost® I-4 engine generating 300 horsepower, mated to an engaging 7-Speed Manual Transmission. This standard setup includes a highly capable 4x4 drivetrain and the Terrain Management System featuring five G.O.A.T. Modes® to easily tackle any landscape. Those seeking heavier performance can upgrade to the 2.7L Ecoboost® V6 Engine, which outputs 330 horsepower through a smooth 10-Speed Automatic Transmission and adds seven G.O.A.T. Modes® alongside the HOSS 4.0 suspension with FOX™ Live Valve 3.1 Internal Bypass Semi-Active Dampers. For maximum thrills, the massive 3.0L Ecoboost® V6 Engine delivers a staggering 418 horsepower.

Comfort inside the 2026 Ford Bronco® Plymouth, MA, perfectly balances durability and convenience. Standard air conditioning, cloth seats, and a Tilt and Telescoping Steering Column ensure a pleasant ride. For true outdoor enthusiasts, the available Washout Capable Rubberized Flooring with Drain Plugs makes post-trail cleanup simple. Additional luxury options include 10-way power driver seats, premium leather upholstery, heated front seats, Dual-Zone Electronic Automatic Temperature Control, and a Safe Deposit Console Lock Box to secure valuables.

The commanding silhouette of the 2026 Ford Bronco® Plymouth, MA, is built for maximum versatility. Standard features include Removable Doors and Top, a Carbonized Gray Molded-in-Color Hard Top for two-door models, and a Manual Swing-Gate with up to 150-degree swing. The aggressive front end showcases LED Headlamps and a Black Molded-in-Color grille featuring bold WHITE Bronco® Lettering. Practical additions like power-heated mirrors, black door handles, and three Tow Hooks prepare you for the trail. Drivers can further customize their ride with 17-inch Black High Gloss-Painted Alloy wheels, Full Underbody Bash Plates, a Heavy-Duty Modular Front Bumper, and Raptor®-Style Running Boards.

The cabin of the 2026 Ford Bronco® Plymouth, MA, acts as a highly connected digital basecamp. A massive 12-inch LCD Capacitive center display with Swipe Capability runs the advanced SYNC® 4 interface, seamlessly integrating Wireless Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™. The driver stays informed via a 12-inch LCD Digital Instrument Cluster. To enhance your journey, available upgrades include a 12-Speaker B&O® Sound System, Pro Power Onboard™ 400 W, and navigation.

Safety is equally advanced, offering standard Pre-Collision Assist with Automatic Emergency Braking, Hill Start Assist, Post Collision Braking, and a Rear View Camera. Highway travel is made easier with optional Adaptive Cruise Control, Evasive Steering Assist, the Lane-Keeping System, and a 360-Degree Camera.
