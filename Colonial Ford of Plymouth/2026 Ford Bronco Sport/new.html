Advanced 4x4 Options

Available Advanced 4x4 with twin clutch rear-drive unit can send torque to a single rear wheel—even when the other three wheels have no traction. Then, it can divert virtually all rear axle torque to either rear wheel. It works in tandem with your selectable G.O.A.T. Modes® (Goes Over Any Type of Terrain).

G.O.A.T. Modes®

Different environments. Different surfaces. Different weather. Adjust your drive mode to match, with standard G.O.A.T. Modes® Goes Over Any Type of Terrain. Select from five standard modes: Normal, ECO, Sport, Slippery, and Off-Road. Bronco Sport® Badlands® model come equipped with two additional modes: Rock Crawl and Rally.*

Ground Clearance

Put some space between yourself and the rough stuff. When properly equipped with available all-terrain off-road tires, achieve an impressive 8.8 inches of ground clearance.

Trail 1-Pedal Driving

On inclines, concentrate more on navigating. Let this available feature* transfer braking control to the accelerator pedal. Whenever you lift your foot, the brakes kick in automatically. Press down, and the brakes release. It also configures drive mode settings as needed, for optimal off-road driving.

Bilstein Shocks

Put the beat-down on bouncy terrain. Available Blistein rear shocks are designed for increased capability to absorb extreme obstacles.

Water Fording

Bronco Sport® SUV, ahoy! When properly equipped, leave dry land behind with an available 24 inches of water-fording capability.*

Ford Trail Control™

Available Ford Trail Control™ allows you to focus on steering during low-speed and off-road use. It intuitively controls acceleration and braking. Use this feature at speeds under 20 MPH, and under 5 MPH in reverse.*

Available Locking Rear Differential

In fresh mud or deep snow, you need traction. And plenty of it. When activated, each rear wheel spins with the same speed—to deliver equal torque.

Trail and Split View Camera

The available 360-Degree Camera with Trail View is activated when in Off-Road and Rally G.O.A.T. Modes® Goes Over Any Type of Terrain. It displays width-accurate tire overlays to help navigate tight spots. Standard on Badlands® model and optional on Outer Banks® model with Outer Banks® Tech package.

Steel Skid Plates

Every trail can get treacherous. Keep vulnerable underbody components protected with available steel skid plates. Durable fenders stand up to rock and stone projectiles too.

Recovery Hooks

If you or a fellow off-roader get stuck, just break out the chains. Two available recovery hooks are located in the front, with two more in the rear.*

Ford Protect®

When added to select Ford Protect® plans, Off-Road Coverage can help cover tire and wheel damage, minor chips and cracks to the windshield, and small dents and dings to the exterior body panels that may come with off-roading.

Trailer Sway Control
