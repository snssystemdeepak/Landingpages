Q&A About the 2027 Ford Expedition ®

1. What kind of engine does the 2027 Ford Expedition have?

This Ford SUV comes standard with a reliable 3.5-liter EcoBoost® V6 that makes 400 horsepower. If you need more pulling power, there is an optional High-Output version that kicks out 440 horsepower and 510 lb.-ft. of torque.

2. How big are the screens inside?

They are huge! You get a 24-inch panoramic display that stretches across the dashboard, plus a 13.2-inch center touchscreen to control all your apps, navigation, and climate settings.

3. Does the tailgate open automatically?

Yes, it comes with a highly convenient Power Upper Liftgate and a Power Lower Tailgate, which makes throwing your gear in the back so much easier when your hands are full.

4. How many seats are in the 2027 Ford Expedition interior?

The 2027 Ford Expedition Plymouth, MA, comes standard with seating for eight people. If your family prefers a little more room to walk to the back row, you can opt for the second-row captain's chairs, which brings the seating capacity to seven.

5. Can you get hands-free driving on the 2027 Ford Expedition Plymouth, MA?

You sure can! Ford offers their BlueCruise® hands-free driving tech as an option, which is awesome for taking the stress out of long highway road trips.
