PERFORMANCE

HARMONIOUS HANDLING

TECHNOLOGY

INTUITIVE, IMMERSIVE, FLEXIBLE.

Stay connected and focused with an available 15.6-inch center touchscreen display, the largest ever offered on a MAZDA vehicle. Our HMI philosophy delivers multiple easy-to-use options for interacting with the vehicle, including the touchscreen, tactile steering wheel controls and voice controls.

DESIGN

THE MOST SPACIOUS MAZDA CX-5 YET

The 2026 MAZDA CX-5 has been redesigned inside and out, with a silhouette that incorporates the KODO:"Soul of Motion" design philosophy to create a sense of movement. The MAZDA signature wing, front grille, angular headlight elements and available 19-inch wheels enhance its appearance, stance and stability.

Inside, the minimalistic interior is designed to be a calm, functional space to help the driver focus, while rear passengers enjoy increased leg, knee and headroom. Premium materials and finishes, as well as available ambient cabin lighting in the door panels, create a refined, curated atmosphere and a more comfortable ride.

 

NEW NAVY BLUE MICA

The 2026 MAZDA CX-5 is available in a vivid and eye-catching new color, Navy Blue Mica. This color is achieved through an intricate process that uses mica particles to accentuate the vehicle’s commanding presence, perfectly complementing the relaxed atmosphere inside the vehicle.

 

SAFETY

CONFIDENCE AND FOCUS
