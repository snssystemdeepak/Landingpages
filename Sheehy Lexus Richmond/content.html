Enhanced handling

Offered on IS 350 F SPORT models, an updated Adaptive Variable Suspension system provides up to 650 levels of active damping. Every movement, tuned in real time. The result is sharper handling, smoother response and a level of driver confidence that redefines the sport sedan experience.

Merging form and function, the 19-inch wheels enhance performance through a new lightweight casting process. And their staggered-width wheel arrangement helps optimize traction, responsiveness and your feeling of control.

Striking in design, the redesigned rear spoiler enhances grip and stability for a more refined, exhilarating drive. Inside, aluminum paddle shifters enable you to control the eight-speed Sport Direct-Shift transmission to deliver lightning-fast upshifts and heart-racing split-second downshifts with rev-matching.

 

Available Triple-Beam LED headlamps punctuate the aggressive new front fascia design. And the rear valance showcases the new lightweight exhaust system, whose visceral engine note with distinctive off-throttle burble work in concert to deliver this sedan's powerful sonic signature in Sport S+ mode.

CONNECTIVITY

User Profiles

The Profiles* feature can save your preferred audio, navigation and language settings—and so much more. Sync via the Lexus app* for seamless personalization across vehicles. 4G network dependent.

Intelligent Assistant

You speak. Intelligent Assistant* understands. Just say "Hey Lexus" to adjust temperature, navigation, audio and more. Active Drive Connect* trial required. 4G network dependent.

Cloud Navigation

Cloud Navigation* uses real-time data to provide you with more precise, turn-by-turn directions. Active Drive Connect* trial required. 4G network dependent.

Destination Assist

Get 24-hour access to a live agent who can help direct you to addresses, restaurants, hotels and more—and send directions to your Cloud Navigation.* Active Drive Connect* trial required. 4G network dependent.

Remote Connect

Remote Connect*. Remotely start your Lexus, lock and unlock doors, check vehicle status and more—all through the Lexus app on your compatible smartphone. 3-year trial included. 4G network dependent.

Wi-Fi Connect

Now everyone can stay connected. Wi-Fi Connect* (AT&T hotspot) allows you to connect up to five Wi-Fi–enabled devices to your vehicle's onboard 4G LTE hotspot. Up to 30-day/3GB trial included. 4G network dependent.

Safety Connect

Find more peace of mind at the touch of a button Safety Connect*. For help in emergency or urgent situations, simply press the in-vehicle SOS button to connect with our 24/7 response center. Minimum 5-year trial subscription included. 4G network dependent.

Service Connect

Use Service Connect* to access Vehicle Health Reports/Alerts, schedule maintenance services at your preferred Lexus dealer and more—all from your preferred connected device. Minimum 5-year trial subscription included. 4G network dependent.

LEXUS SAFETY SYSTEM+ 3.0

2026 IS models now include Lexus Safety System+ 3.0,* a comprehensive suite of advanced active safety equipment and convenience features designed to bring peace of mind to every drive.

PRE-COLLISION SYSTEM WITH PEDESTRIAN DETECTION

Pre-Collision System with Pedestrian Detection* can alert you to certain potential collisions and may automatically brake.

FULL-SPEED RANGE DYNAMIC RADAR CRUISE CONTROL

Full-Speed Range Dynamic Radar Cruise Control* helps maintain a preset distance from the vehicle ahead of you, from highway speeds down to a full stop.

LANE DEPARTURE ALERT WITH STEERING ASSIST

Lane Departure Alert with Steering Assist* may alert you to inadvertent lane departures and make minor steering corrections to help keep you in your lane.

LANE TRACING ASSIST

Lane Tracing Assist* helps you center the vehicle within its lane while Dynamic Radar Cruise Control* is active.

ROAD SIGN ASSIST

Road Sign Assist* can recognize certain road signs and display them to you inside the vehicle.

AUTOMATIC HIGH BEAMS

Automatic High Beams* can automatically toggle between high and low beams based on the vehicle's surroundings.

 

PROACTIVE DRIVING ASSIST

Proactive Driving Assist* may provide gentle braking and/or steering to help control distance between your vehicle and a vehicle, pedestrian, or bicyclist ahead of you.
