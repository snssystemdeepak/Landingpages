CONNECTIVITY

CONNECTIVITY

User Profiles

The Profiles* feature can save your preferred audio, navigation and language settings—and so much more. Sync via the Lexus app for seamless personalization across vehicles. 4G network dependent.

Intelligent Assistant

You speak. Intelligent Assistant* understands. Just say "Hey Lexus" to adjust temperature, navigation, audio and more. Active Drive Connect* trial required. 4G network dependent.

Cloud Navigation

Cloud Navigation* uses real-time data to provide you with more precise, turn-by-turn directions. Active Drive Connect* trial required. 4G network dependent.

Destination Assist

Get 24-hour access to a live agent who can help direct you to addresses, restaurants, hotels and more—and send directions to your Cloud Navigation.* Active Drive Connect* trial required. 4G network dependent.

Remote Connect

Remote Connect*. Remotely start your Lexus, lock and unlock doors, check vehicle status and more—all through the Lexus app on your compatible smartphone. 3-year trial included. 4G network dependent.

Wi-Fi Connect

Now everyone can stay connected. Wi-Fi Connect* (AT&T hotspot) allows you to connect up to five Wi-Fi–enabled devices to your vehicle's onboard 4G LTE hotspot. Up to 30-day/3GB trial included. 4G network dependent.

Safety Connect

Find more peace of mind at the touch of a button with Safety Connect*. For help in emergency or urgent situations, simply press the in-vehicle SOS button to connect with our 24/7 response center. Minimum 5-year trial subscription included. 4G network dependent.

Service Connect

Access Vehicle Health Reports/Alerts,* schedule maintenance services at your preferred Lexus dealer and more—all from your preferred connected device. 4G network dependent.

CONVENIENCE

Digital Rearview Mirror

This available system utilizes a rear-mounted camera to display a high-resolution view of the road behind you. Simply toggle the lever to switch between traditional and digital mirror modes.

Digital Latch with Safe Exit Assist

When parked, this system* can monitor surroundings for oncoming vehicles or bicyclists, and can prevent the door from opening until conditions are determined to be clear.

DIGITAL KEY

The available Digital Key* feature enables you to lock, unlock and start your vehicle via the Lexus app on your smartphone. You can also share access with up to seven family members or friends for added convenience. Remote Connect trial required. 4G network dependent.

Panoramic View Monitor

Four available high-resolution cameras mounted on the front, sides and rear of the vehicle give drivers a bird's-eye view of the near environment.* Moving View and See-Through View options create a composite image of the vehicle's surroundings as on-screen guides help assist with parking and tight maneuvering.

USB-C Charging Ports

USB-C charging ports* found in every row make it easy for everyone to power their favorite electronic devices.
