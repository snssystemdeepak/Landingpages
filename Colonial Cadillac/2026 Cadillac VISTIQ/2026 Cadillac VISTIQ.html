HOME ENERGY SOLUTIONS WITH VEHICLE-TO-HOME TECHNOLOGY

• What is Vehicle-to-Home (V2H) technology and how does it work?
Vehicle-to-Home (V2H) technology allows your compatible electric vehicle to act as a mobile power bank for your properly equipped home during a power outage.* By connecting your compatible EV to your home energy system, you can use the energy stored in the vehicle’s battery to power select household appliances during outages. This smart integration helps provide energy availability during emergencies.

• Can I use my EV to power my home during a blackout?
Your compatible GM EV can provide power to your properly equipped home in a blackout.*

• What do I need to set up Vehicle-to-Home (V2H) functionality in my home?
To enable V2H functionality, you’ll need a GM Energy PowerShift Charger and GM Energy V2H Enablement Kit to move energy between your compatible EV and your properly equipped home.*
