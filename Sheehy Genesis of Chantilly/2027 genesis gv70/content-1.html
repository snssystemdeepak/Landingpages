DESIGN

Sculpted for presence. Shaped for motion. The GV70’s athletic proportions and refined details create an unmistakable profile that conveys effortless refinement.

TWO-LINE LED HEADLAMPS

The GV70’s showstopping front profile is flanked by the Genesis signature Two-Line LED headlamps with Micro Lens Array technology for exceptional brilliance.

SPORTY 21” WHEELS

The GV70’s athleticism is further accentuated by available 21” contemporary sport wheels inspired by the iconic Genesis Two-Line design signature.

RACECAR INSPIRED STEERING

Engineered for performance driving, the available D-Cut Steering Wheel provides sporty comfort and responsive control—all in the palms of your hands.

SINK INTO NAPPA LEATHER

Experience the soft luxury of available Nappa Leather and tailor-like stitching which flows throughout the cabin seating.

 

SET THE VIBE

Enhance your mood with standard Adjustable Interior Ambient Lighting. Select a color and personalize the aura of the cabin to customize your ride.

 

TECHNOLOGY

The GV70 is outfitted with intuitive intelligence, transforming every drive into one that’s effortlessly connected—all accessible through a 27” OLED Display with wireless Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™ compatibility*.

Bang & Olufsen® PREMIUM AUDIO

The GV70’s available Bang & Olufsen® Premium Audio sound system* delivers sonically rich and symphonic surround sound that you don’t just hear—you feel.

FOCUSED ON THE ROAD

Keep your eyes safely on the road while accessing vital driving data right from your windshield. The GV70’s available 12” Head-Up Display conveniently projects speed, directions, safety functionality, and more.

QI WIRELESS CHARGING

Conveniently charge your compatible smartphone on the go without cables*. Simply place your phone on the available clutter-free pad, and it will stay charged so you can stay connected.

GO KEYLESS

With available Digital Key 2* loaded onto your compatible smartphone or smartwatch, you can conveniently unlock, lock and start your GV70 without touching the door. You can even remotely control certain functions, like opening or closing the liftgate.

 

RELAX INTO COMFORT

RELAX INTO COMFORT

Crafted for restorative comfort, the GV70 features an available Ergo Motion driver seat which uses built-in air cells to provide on-demand lumbar stretching to help reduce fatigue over long drives.

 

PERFORMANCE

Beneath its sporty exterior lies unmistakable control—on and off the road. With the GV70’s Terrain Mode, you can conquer snow, mud, and sand with confidence, and enjoy performance that feels confident and composed.

ON-DEMAND POWER

Activate Sport+ Mode for an exhilarating performance-driven connection with the road. Acceleration intensifies, throttle response sharpens, and stability control intervention is reduced for a more hands-on feeling.

TURBOCHARGED ENGINES

Experience pure exhilaration from the GV70’s standard 300-HP* 2.5 liter turbocharged inline 4-cylinder. Or unleash an available 375-HP* Twin-Turbo V6.

SMOOTH DRIVING AHEAD

Available Electronically Controlled Suspension with Road Preview* predictively reads the road with the front camera and automatically adjusts the suspension for a seamless and comfortable ride.

 

ELECTRONIC LIMITED SLIP DIFFERENTIAL

ELECTRONIC LIMITED SLIP DIFFERENTIAL

An available Rear Electronic Limited Slip Differential* lets you feel the road by intelligently distributing power between the left and right rear wheels to help maintain grip and precision control when cornering and navigating slick surfaces.

 

SAFETY

Confidence comes standard with a comprehensive suite of driver assistance features, advanced cameras, and safety technologies designed to help protect you on every journey.

FORWARD COLLISION-AVOIDANCE ASSIST

Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist* can help detect certain stopped vehicles and stationary objects in front of you and can issue an alert or even apply the brakes when necessary.

INTELLIGENT LANE MONITORING

The GV70 helps you change lanes with confidence. When you activate the turn signal, the available Blind-Spot View Monitor displays a live video feed from the side-view camera*, and Blind-Spot Collision-Avoidance Assist can alert you to vehicles in your blind spots*

FOCUS ON THE ROAD

Driver Attention Warning* can help you stay focused and alert by monitoring driving patterns and informing you when you might need a break. Stopped at a red light? It can even let you know when the vehicle ahead of you begins to move. Forward Attention Warning* monitors the driver's eye direction and displays a warning message if you need to refocus on the road ahead.

DESIGNED FOR REAR PASSENGERS

Like an extra pair of eyes, available Advanced Rear Occupant Alert* uses high-precision radar to help detect if a child or pet is present in the back seat and reminds the driver when exiting the vehicle.

KEEPING YOU ENGAGED

When driver assistance systems, such as Highway Driving Assist* are activated, Hands-On Detection* can issue warnings if your hands aren’t detected on the steering wheel, helping to keep you alert behind the wheel.

 

ELECTRIFIED GV70

The Electrified GV70 asserts its presence with a dynamic stance and instant all-electric power, redefining the norms for EV style and innovation.

Charging

From its discreetly integrated port to its fast-charging capability and extensive network access, the Electrified GV70 keeps electric driving simple.

UP TO 250 MILES

The Electrified GV70’s 84-kWh battery’s 250-mile range* further untethers you from the charging station to confidently pursue the scenic route or a weekend escape.

LESS WAITING. MORE DRIVING.

Power up from 10% to 80% in as few as 19* minutes, using an 800V DC Ultra-Fast Charger. Drawing on up to 350kW of power, these quick chargers help reduce your down time to get you back on the road sooner.

AN INNOVATIVE PLUG IN

The North American Charging Standard (NACS) port is heated against cold-weather frost and uses subtle lighting elements to display the current level of charge. When charging is complete, activate the electric access door, and it disappears into the clean lines of the grille.

MOBILE POWER SOURCE

The Electrified GV70 features an available vehicle-to-load adapter which transforms your vehicle’s charging port into a grille-mounted 110-volt outlet*.

A FULL ENERGY SYSTEM

Genesis Home* is a complete clean energy solution for your Electrified GV70. Giving you the ability to produce, use and store solar energy with state-of-the-art solar panels, a 240-volt Level 2 ChargePoint® Home Flex EV Charger* and highly efficient storage systems.
