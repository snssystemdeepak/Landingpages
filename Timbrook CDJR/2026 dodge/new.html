SAFETY & SECURITY

CONFIDENCE COMES STANDARD

Safety and security are built in, not just added on. The 2026 Dodge Durango is engineered to deliver added peace of mind, mile after mile with standard safety and security features.

BLIND SPOT MONITORING WITH REAR CROSS PATH DETECTION*

This helpful safety and security feature uses sensors to detect vehicles in your blind spot zone or when backing up. The system will alert you when a vehicle enters your blind spot, or when vehicles cross your path as you move in reverse.

Parkview® REAR BACK UP CAMERA

When parking, this system provides a high-resolution view behind your SUV while also displaying dynamic gridlines to help guide your vehicle as you move in reverse.

ELECTRONIC STABILITY CONTROL

An automatic vehicle safety system that helps prevent skidding or loss of control by detecting when your SUV is not moving in the direction you intend, and then selectively applying the brakes to individual wheels and/or reducing engine power to help keep the vehicle stable.

ADVANCED BRAKE ASSIST WITH READY ALERT BRAKING*

Advanced Brake Assist provides additional braking power when a sudden stop is needed. Ready-Alert Braking senses that the driver is about to release the accelerator abruptly and prepares the brakes for sudden stopping.

MORE REINFORCEMENTS

A collection of available advanced sensors, cameras and integrated technology delivers safety, security and added peace of mind behind the wheel.

BLIND SPOT MONITORING WITH TRAILER TOW DETECTION

The 2026 Dodge Durango doesn’t just tow with power—it tows with confidence. The available Blind Spot Monitoring* with Trailer Tow Detection System, included in the Trailer Tow Package, alerts you when something’s in your vehicle’s blind spot or next to your trailer when changing lanes.

LaneSense® LANE DEPARTURE WARNING WITH LANE KEEP ASSIST

When an unintentional lane drift is detected, available LaneSense® Lane Departure Warning with Lane Keep Assist* can provide audible and visual alerts, and can even help you stay centered in your lane with steering assistance.

FORWARD COLLISION WARNING WITH FULL STOP

Available Forward Collision Warning with Full Stop* uses audible and visible alerts to let the driver know when a forward collision is incoming, and can even use automated emergency braking to bring the vehicle to a full stop as needed.

ADAPTIVE CRUISE CONTROL WITH STOP

Available Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop* automatically adjusts your vehicle’s speed to maintain a safe distance from the vehicle in front of it. If necessary, the system can even bring your vehicle to a complete stop.
