Performance

What's Under the Hood Makes All the Difference

The 2025 lineup of F-150® pickups are giants of capability and performance courtesy of an array of mighty engine choices. Each one is engineered with impressive power ratings including the powerfully proficient 3.5L PowerBoost® Full Hybrid V6 engine and high-performance 5.2L Supercharged V8 engine.

Remarkably Strong Muscle Group

Whatever you do with your F-150® pickup — from towing a trailer to blazing over a sand dune — there's an engine designed to do just that. That's because we've engineered six immensely capable engines to easily handle the things you do.

3.5L PowerBoost® Full-Hybrid V6

The available, 3.5L PowerBoost® Full Hybrid V6 engine is making a performance statement with the collective might of gas and electric energy. Its impressive power comes through with 430 horsepower, 570 lb.- ft. of torque and a maximum 11,200 lbs. of towing capability.* To top it off, it has a heavy-lifting maximum available payload rating of 2,120 lbs.*

3.5L EcoBoost® V6

The available 3.5L EcoBoost® V6 delivers power ratings of 400 horsepower and 500 lb.-ft. of torque. it also carries with it an available tow rating of 13,500 lbs.* and 2,440 lbs. payload rating.* Features include the Ford port-fuel and direct-injection (PFDI) system with two injectors per cylinder to increase performance. Plus, twin intercooled turbos with virtually no lag.

2.7L EcoBoost® V6

Engineered with the Ford port-fuel and direct-injection (PFDI) system, dual overhead cam design with variable cam timing, plus twin intercooled turbochargers for on-demand power, the 2.7 EcoBoost® delivers a robust 325 horsepower and 400 lb.-ft. of torque.*

5.0L Ti-VCT V8

This legendary available Ford V8 delivers all the muscle you need to haul a maximum payload rating of 3,310 lbs.* Experience robust 400 horsepower and 410 lb.-ft. of torque,* courtesy of twin independent variable camshaft timing (Ti-VCT), as well as the Ford port-fuel and direct-injection (PFDI) system with two injectors per cylinder for increased power.

Accelerate productivity with engineered solutions to help do your job more efficiently with Ford Pro™.

3.5L High Output EcoBoost® V6

The 3.5L Twin-Turbo HO EcoBoost® engine, only available on the F-150® Raptor® pickup, provides the punch behind the Baja-bred Raptor®. Its extraordinary power is fed via the Ford port-fuel and direct-injection (PFDI) system generating an enormous 450 horsepower and 510 lb.-ft. of torque.*

5.2L Supercharged V8

The most powerful high-performance engine ever in an F-150® truck. With pin-you-to-your-seat supercharged 720 horsepower and 640 lb.-ft. of torque,* you're well equipped to leave your mark at every light.

3.5L PowerBoost® Full Hybrid V6

Sometimes it runs on gas, sometimes electricity — or a combination of both. Either way, the available hybrid engine generates marked performance and astounding capability whether on the job or vacation.

Tandem Force of Capability
The 3.5L PowerBoost® Hybrid engine is a two-way powerhouse. Harnessing the combined efforts of gas combustion and electric energy to achieve maximum towing capacity of 11,200 lbs.,* 430 horsepower and 570 lb.-ft. of torque.*

Amped Up Productivity
Not only will you get exceptional performance on the road, but with the 3.5L PowerBoost® hybrid engine, you can also achieve great power in the truck bed with up to 7.2kW of available electrical power from the Pro Power Onboard.*

Built Ford Tough®

When the day runs long and everyone else is punching their card, F-150® trucks keep powering on until the job is complete. It can, because it’s made of a high-strength steel frame and military-grade aluminum alloy. And every component is engineered to endure just about anything you put it through.

Technology

The Tech You Need When You Need It

Wherever your F-150® truck takes you, you’ll have ingenious convenience riding along. With modern tech features like the available Ford BlueCruise* hands-free highway driving to high-speed 5G hotspot connectivity and Ford Co-Pilot360® driver-assist features.*

Hands-Free Highway Driving

Ford BlueCruise* is an available advanced driver-assistance feature that enables hands-free highway driving, helping make your drive less stressful and more enjoyable. Whether you're in stop-and-go traffic or on a long road trip, BlueCruise controls the steering, braking, acceleration, and lane-centering. A driver-facing camera tracks eye gaze and head position to help ensure your eyes stay on the road while your hands are off the wheel. BlueCruise works collaboratively with the driver, helping reduce the fatigue of driving, allowing you to arrive more refreshed and energized at your destination.

Consumer Reports named BlueCruise its top-rated Active Driving Assistance System two times in a row, with BlueCruise receiving the highest score in the categories of "capabilities and performance", "keeping the driver engaged", and "clear when safe to use".*

Towing & Hauling

Move Big and Heavy Things With Ease

The bigger the job the bigger the muscle to get it done. Whether hauling bricks or pulling the family trailer, F-150® trucks deliver outstanding towing and hauling capability. That's why our trucks are called upon to get you where you need to be — with what you need.

Outstanding Payload Capacity

The F-150® pickup's body and bed are built of lightweight, high-strength, military-grade aluminum alloy. Now add immense strength from a fully-boxed high-strength steel frame and you get an available payload rating of 2,440 lbs.*

Immense Towing Capability

There’s no time for ifs, ands or buts when it comes to towing large trailers, so you look to the truck with the muscle to do it. The available 3.5L EcoBoost® V6 engine delivers 13,500 lbs.* of available towing capacity.

Takes Smarts to Tow Smart

With so many demands placed upon a truck’s capability, you need a truck that can handle it — while being rich with towing technology. That’s where the F-150® comes ready with smart integrated features to help hitch, maneuver and tow with confidence.

Pro Trailer Backup Assist™

Whether you’re a novice or a seasoned pro, backing up with a trailer can be challenging. Available Pro Trailer Backup Assist™ makes it as easy as turning a knob — simply rotate in the direction you want the trailer to go and it'll move your truck and trailer into position.*

Pro Trailer Hitch Assist™

Perfect for when it’s just you and your pickup. Simply press and hold the available Pro Trailer Hitch Assist™ button and it automatically takes over. It’ll steer, accelerate and brake for you as it guides your truck into place for easy hitching to your conventional trailer.*

BLIS® with Trailer Coverage

Changing lanes while towing a trailer can be daunting. BLIS® (Blind Spot Information System®) with Trailer Coverage can help by alerting you not only when a vehicle is detected in your blind spot, but also when one is alongside your trailer.*

Cleared to Tow

The available Smart Trailer Tow Connector informs you if something regarding your trailer needs attention. Be alerted if your tow or turn indicator lamps are out or there are brake lamps or trailer battery problems via the tow activity screen.

Hauling with Confidence

Adaptive Suspension is fully automatic depending on the drive mode you select and dynamically adjusts shock absorber stiffness in real time to match the road surface and driver inputs. By monitoring suspension position, vehicle loading, vehicle speed, road conditions and steering, it regulates suspension damping for enhanced vehicle control, particularly when towing or carrying heavy loads.
