Seven Available Drive Modes

Experience your drive. Your way. There are seven different available drive modes, including Normal, Eco, Sport, Tow/Haul, Slippery, Trail, and when equipped, Deep Snow/Sand. Once selected, these modes automatically modify the electronic stability control system and traction control system for optimized performance.

SelectShift® Capability

Turn your automatic transmission into an on-demand manual one with conveniently placed paddle shifters with SelectShift® capability. Only available on the ST model.

10-Speed Transmission
