Available LaneSense® Lane Departure Warning with Lane Keep Assist* can provide audible and visual alerts when an unintentional lane drift is detected. The feature can also provide steering input to help keep your vehicle from drifting out of its lane.

BLIND SPOT MONITORING WITH TRAILER TOW DETECTION

Even the incredible towing capacity of the 2025 Dodge DURANGO is protected with the available Blind Spot Monitoring* with Trailer Tow Detection System, included in the available Trailer Tow Package. When the driver engages the turn signal, the system alerts them if a vehicle is in their blind spot or on the side of their trailer. Those big hauls just got a lot easier.

FORWARD COLLISION WARNING WITH FULL STOP

Available Forward Collision Warning with Full Stop* uses audible and visible alerts to let the driver know when a forward collision is incoming, and can even use automated emergency braking to bring the vehicle to a full stop as needed.

ADAPTIVE CRUISE CONTROL WITH STOP

The available Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop* feature uses sensor input to keep the DURANGO a predetermined distance from the vehicle in front of it. If necessary, the system can even bring the vehicle to a complete stop.
