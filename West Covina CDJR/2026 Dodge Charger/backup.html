Standard on Daytona Scat Pack, Race Prep lets drivers choose one of two racing styles. Drag Racing optimizes battery temperature to avoid derate and deliver quick acceleration off the line. Standard on Daytona Scat Pack, Drift/Donut Mode sets you up for the thrill of drifting and donuts using three retractable slip angles, rear axle-biased torque and tailored damper settings.
