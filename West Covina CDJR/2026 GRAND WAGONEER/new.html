Entering and exiting difficult parking spots is easy with advanced sensors that help detect parking spots and steer the vehicle while you use gear selection, throttle and braking to place your vehicle right between the lines.

Grand Wagoneer uses intelligent sensors to track pedestrians and cyclists you may not see and automatically applies the brakes if necessary.

Active Lane Management System

The Active Lane Management System* reads the road as you travel. If your vehicle begins to drift to either side, it can deliver subtle steering wheel input to help guide you back to the center of your lane.

Blind Spot Monitoring* and Rear Cross Path Detection* use radar-equipped sensors to detect either vehicles in your blind spot zones or those crossing behind you when you’re moving in reverse.
