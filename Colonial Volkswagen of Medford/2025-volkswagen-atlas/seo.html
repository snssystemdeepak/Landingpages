Move to higher level configurations at our VW dealership for navigation, remote engine start, a head-up display, road sign display and a Harman Kardon® premium audio system. When it comes to safety, the 2025 Volkswagen Atlas Boston, MA, outshines the competition with a premier suite of driver assistance technologies. Standard safety features include forward collision warnings, rear cross traffic alerts, lane assist, emergency assist and travel assist. In addition, this VW SUV is equipped with autonomous emergency braking with pedestrian and cyclist monitoring, a lane change system and an advanced airbag system.
