Available upgrades include Climatronic® dual-zone automatic climate control, leather seating surfaces, ambient lighting, heated and ventilated front seats and an 8-way power driver seat with lumbar support.

Technology features in the 2026 Volkswagen Taos Boston, MA, focus on clean connectivity. Exterior styling for the 2026 Volkswagen Taos Boston, MA, balances sleek design with functional equipment. Standard 17-inch alloy wheels pair with auto on/off LED projector headlights for strong visibility. Black exterior styling elements, black roof rails and connected LED taillights with an illuminated rear VW badge give the VW Taos a bold look day or night. Power mirrors and rain-sensing variable intermittent windshield wipers add convenience in unpredictable weather. Optional enhancements include 19-inch alloy wheels, a power tilting and sliding panoramic sunroof and an integrated light bar in the front grille. Safety is a strong foundation of the 2026 Volkswagen Taos Boston, MA.
