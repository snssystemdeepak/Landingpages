Safety & Convenience

Standard Blind Spot Monitor

Standard Blind Spot Monitor (BSM)* is designed to warn you when a vehicle in the next lane enters your vehicle's blind spot on either side of the vehicle.

Ten Airbags

Corolla comes standard with ten airbags,* including the driver and front passenger Advanced Airbag System, driver and front passenger seat-mounted side airbags, driver knee airbag, front passenger seat cushion airbag, rear seat-mounted side airbags, and front and rear side curtain airbags.

Star Safety System™

Standard on every new Corolla is our Star Safety System™, an advanced integration of safety technologies designed to enhance traction, braking and more. Armed with these safety features, Corolla helps you avoid accidents in the first place.

No Cost Maintenance Plan and Roadside Assistance

Every new Toyota comes with ToyotaCare,* a maintenance plan that covers initial normal factory scheduled service and 24-hour Roadside Assistance.

Toyota Safety Sense™ 3.0*

PCS w/PD* is designed to alert you to potential collisions and may automatically brake.

Full-Speed Range Dynamic Radar Cruise Control

DRCC* is designed to maintain a preset distance from the vehicle ahead of you, from highway speeds down to a full stop.

Lane Departure Alert With Steering Assist

LDA w/SA* is designed to alert you to inadvertent lane departures and make minor steering corrections to help keep you in your lane.

Lane Tracing Assist

LTA* is designed to center the vehicle within its lane while DRCC* is active.

Road Sign Assist

RSA* is designed to recognize certain road signs and display them to you inside the vehicle.

Automatic High Beams

AHB* is designed to automatically toggle between high and low beams based on the vehicle’s surroundings.

Proactive Driving Assist

PDA* is designed to provide gentle braking and/or steering to help control distance between your vehicle and a vehicle, pedestrian or bicyclist ahead of you.
