The 2026 Volkswagen Golf GTI Boston, MA, utilizes a 2.0L TSI® 16-valve DOHC turbo 4-cylinder engine pushing 241 horsepower and 273 lb.-ft. of torque via a quick-shifting 7-speed DSG® automatic transmission. This VW hot hatch features Driving Mode Selection and striking red brake calipers with vented rotors. For uncompromising power, the 2026 Volkswagen Golf R Boston, MA, turns up the heat with a massive 328 horsepower and 295 lb.-ft. of torque from its tuned 2.0L TSI® motor. The 2026 VW Golf R Boston, MA, adds standard R-Performance 4MOTION® all-wheel drive with Torque Vectoring, as well as Multimode DCC and Adaptive Chassis Control (an available option for the Golf GTI).

The athletic stance of both VW models is designed to command attention. The 2026 Volkswagen Golf GTI Boston, MA, comes equipped with 18" alloy wheels, LED fog lights, and black side window trim. The 2026 Volkswagen Golf R Boston, MA, rides on massive 19" forged alloy wheels with summer performance tires and includes a standard Power tilting and sliding panoramic sunroof. Both Volkswagen hatchbacks feature auto on/off LED headlights, the Adaptive Front Lighting System (AFS) with Dynamic Cornering Lights, a Front grille-integrated light bar, an illuminated VW logo, and illuminated door handle recesses.

Confidence is deeply integrated into both VW hatchbacks. The 2026 Volkswagen Golf GTI Boston, MA, and the 2026 Volkswagen Golf R Boston, MA, share a robust safety suite. Standard systems include an Active Blind Spot Monitor with RTA, Adaptive Cruise Control, and Dynamic Road Sign Display. The advanced Front Assist provides Forward Collision Warnings and Autonomous Emergency Braking with Pedestrian and Cyclist Monitoring, while Travel Assist, Lane Assist, and Emergency Assist keep you secure on the highway.

Step into a fully digitized cabin tailored for the driving enthusiast. At the center is the Discover Pro 12.9" touchscreen with App-Connect®, Apple CarPlay®, Android Auto™, and connected services from myVW+. Drivers view key metrics on the Volkswagen Digital Cockpit Pro 10.25" configurable digital instrument display. You also get push-button start, 4 USB-C ports, and Ventilated 15W wireless charging. The 2026 Volkswagen Golf R Boston, MA, includes a standard Head-Up Display, Park Assist Plus, and a Harman Kardon® premium audio system—premium features that remain available as options on the 2026 Volkswagen Golf GTI Boston, MA.

The five-passenger cabins offer supreme comfort. The 2026 Volkswagen Golf GTI Boston, MA, features nostalgic Cloth seats with Scalepaper Plaid inserts, heated front seats, and Climatronic® Touch single-zone climate control. The 2026 Volkswagen Golf R Boston, MA, upgrades the experience with standard leather seats, a 12-way power driver seat, ventilated front seats, heated rear outboard seats, and Climatronic® Touch three-zone automatic climate control. Both vehicles sport Stainless steel pedal caps, an auto-dimming rearview mirror, and multi-color adjustable ambient lighting to set the exact mood you want.
