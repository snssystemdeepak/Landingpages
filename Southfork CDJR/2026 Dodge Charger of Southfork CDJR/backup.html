LINE LOCK

Standard on Charger Scat Pack, Line Lock lets the driver lock the front brakes only, while enabling rear wheel spin. When engaged, Line Lock helps clean/warm the tires before launch and perform tire-smoking burnouts with a push of a button and punch of the throttle.

LAUNCH CONTROL

Launch straight forward, right off the line. Standard on Charger Scat Pack and Daytona Scat Pack, Launch Control sets the torque at the wheels offering straight-line acceleration at the starting point and reducing wheel slip based on surface conditions.

RACE PREP

Standard on Daytona Scat Pack, Race Prep lets drivers choose one of two racing styles. Drag Racing optimizes battery temperature to avoid derate and deliver quick acceleration off the line. Track Racing cools the battery temperature, allowing it to heat up gradually and helping to maintain stamina when racing longer laps. Pre-production model shown.

DRIFT/DONUT MODE

Standard on Daytona Scat Pack, Drift/Donut Mode sets you up for the thrill of drifting and donuts using three retractable slip angles, rear axle-biased torque and tailored damper settings. Drift Min provides the most traction control intervention and allows for the minimum slip, Drift Max provides less traction control for more slip and Donut provides the least amount of traction control for the wildest slip of all.

FORWARD COLLISION WARNING PLUS*
