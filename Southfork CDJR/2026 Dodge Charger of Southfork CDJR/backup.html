Active Driving Assist

Active Driving Assist

The semiautonomous Active Driving Assist System* uses an array of cameras and sensors to make subtle adjustments to steering and acceleration so that you can remain a set distance from any vehicle directly ahead and stay centered in your lane.

Intersection Collision Assist

Intersection Collision Assist* uses sensors to detect oncoming vehicles at four-way intersections.

Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop And Go

Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop and Go* automatically adjusts your driving speed to maintain a preselected distance from the vehicle ahead.

Active Lane Management

The Active Lane Management System* measures your position within lane boundaries. If the system detects an unintentional lane drift, it can alert you and correct the vehicle’s position within the lane. The system also provides steering input to help avoid collisions with vehicles in your blind spot zones.

Blind Spot Monitoring and Rear Cross Path Detection

Blind Spot Monitoring* and Rear Cross Path Detection* use radar-equipped sensors to detect either vehicles in your blind spot zones or those crossing behind you when you're moving in reverse.

Full-Speed Forward Collision Warning with Active Braking and Pedestrian/Cyclist Detection

Full-Speed Forward Collision Warning with Active Braking and Pedestrian/Cyclist Detection* uses sensors at the front of the vehicle to detect and alert you to a potential front impact or obstacles you might not see. The vehicle can automatically engage the brakes if you don't respond.

ParkView ® Rear Back Up Camera with ParkSense ® Rear Park Assist

A dedicated rear back up camera allows ParkView®* to show you what’s behind when you’re moving in reverse, while ParkSense® Rear Park Assist* helps put your Grand Cherokee in precisely the right spot by offering audible and visual alerts.
