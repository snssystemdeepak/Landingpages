Safety & Convenience

Blind Spot Monitor With Rear Cross-Traffic Alert

RAV4’s Blind Spot Monitor (BSM)* warns you when a vehicle enters your blind spot and Rear Cross-Traffic Alert (RCTA)* helps detect vehicles approaching from the sides as you reverse. Additionally, Safe Exit Alert (SEA)* helps alert passengers of approaching vehicles or cyclists when a door is opening.

Panoramic View Monitor With 3D View

The Panoramic View Monitor (PVM),* now with 3D view, helps you see multiple angles as you navigate tight turns, narrow spaces and tricky parking.

Drive Recorder

From unexpected moments to everyday drives. The standard Drive Recorder* helps document portions of your journey by capturing forward-facing video under certain conditions.

Eight Airbags

RAV4 comes standard with eight airbags* total, including a driver and front passenger Advanced Airbag System with driver and front passenger seat-mounted side airbags, driver knee airbag, a front passenger seat-cushion airbag, and front and rear side curtain airbags.

Star Safety System™

Every new RAV4 is built with our Star Safety System™, an advanced integration of safety technologies designed to enhance traction, braking and more. Armed with these safety features, RAV4 is designed to keep you out of harm’s way.

No Cost Maintenance Plan and Roadside Assistance

Every new RAV4 comes with ToyotaCare,* a maintenance plan that covers normal factory scheduled service for 2 years or 25,000 miles, whichever comes first, and 24-hour Roadside Assistance for 2 years, unlimited mileage.

TSS 4.0*

Pre-Collision System With Pedestrian Detection

Full-Speed Range Dynamic Radar Cruise Control

DRCC* helps maintain a preset distance from the vehicle ahead of you, from highway speeds down to a full stop.

Automatic High Beams

AHB* can automatically toggle between high and low beams based on the vehicle’s surroundings.

Lane Departure Alert With Steering Assist

LDA w/SA* may alert you to inadvertent lane departures and make minor steering corrections to help keep you in your lane.

Lane Tracing Assist

LTA* helps you center the vehicle within its lane while DRCC* is active.

Road Sign Assist

RSA* can recognize certain road signs and display them to you inside the vehicle.

Proactive Driving Assist

PDA* may provide gentle braking and/or steering to help control distance between your vehicle and a vehicle, pedestrian or bicyclist ahead of you.
