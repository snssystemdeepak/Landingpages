Levels of Capability

Enhanced Off-Roading

This The 3.0L EcoBoost® V6 engine with Auto Start-Stop Technology is the backbone of the ST, King Ranch® and Platinum model powertrains. It combines turbocharging and direct injection to produce 400HP* and 415 lb.-ft. of torque.*

Trail Technology

Trail Technology

Upgrade your off-road experience with optional features like Ford Trail Control™ with 1-Pedal Driving.

G.O.A.T. Modes®

Adjust your drive mode to match the environment. Bronco Sport® standard G.O.A.T. Modes® (Goes Over Any Type of Terrain) features five standard modes: Normal, ECO, Sport, Slippery, and Off-Road. The ever-capable Bronco Sport® Badlands® models comes equipped with two additional modes: Rock Crawl and Rally.

Off-Road Mode

This standard G.O.A.T. mode® helps optimize performance for off-road driving, allowing you to tackle muddy, rutted, or uneven terrains. The standard terrain management system adapts handling, steering, and powertrain for various off-road conditions.

Rally Mode

Stay in control off-road, even in sandy terrain. This mode adjusts shift points and steering, while the optimized throttle control helps enhance responsiveness and torque delivery.

Confidently Go Off Road

There are many features to help elevate your outdoor adventures.

Ground Clearance

If properly equipped with the available all-terrain off-road tires, you can achieve 8.8 inches of ground clearance.

Water Fording
Achieves 24 inches of water-fording capability when equipped.*

Class Exclusive* Trail 1-Pedal Driving
This available terrain-taming feature lets the accelerator pedal takes control. Lift your foot and the brakes kick in automatically. Press down, and the brakes release. It also configures drive mode settings as needed, to optimize your off-road driving experience.

Recovery Hooks
Travel various terrains with confidence knowing you have the proper equipment to get you through. Available recovery hooks are located in the front and two in the rear.

Ford Trail Control™
With available Ford Trail Control™, you focus on steering during low-speed and off-road use by controlling your vehicle’s acceleration and braking. You can use trail control™ under 20 MPH and under 5 MPH in reverse.

Class Exclusive* Bilstein Shocks
The 2025 Bronco Sport® SUV features new available Bilstein rear shocks for increased capability to absorb extreme obstacles.

Steel Skid Plates

Take on the off-road. The available steel skid plates and durable fenders protect the underbody of your vehicle when you’re adventuring.

Available Locking Rear Differential
Each rear wheel spins with the same speed and delivers equal torque.

Class Exclusive Trail and Split View Camera
The available 360-Degree Camera with class-exclusive* Trail View is activated when in Off-Road and Rally G.O.A.T. Modes®, displaying width-accurate tire overlays. Standard on Badlands® model, and optional with Sasquatch® Outer Banks® package.

Tires
For 2025 models, the Sasquatch® Package offers 29" 235/65/R17 Goodyear Territory All Terrain tires, the largest available in the class.*

Off-Roadeo®
The Off-Roadeo® experience connects Bronco owners to the spirit of adventure, offering hands-on experiences that build off-road skills and let drivers fully enjoy their Bronco's capabilities. This exclusive experience is included with your Bronco or Bronco Sport® purchase. They’re several locations to choose from, and you won’t be driving your own vehicle.

Ford Protect™
When added to select Ford Protect™ plans, Off-Road Coverage can help cover tire and wheel damage, minor chips and cracks to the windshield, and small dents and dings to the exterior body panels that may come with off-roading.
