Dual pane sunroof

The available dual pane sunroof spans both the front and rear of PALISADE, offering all passengers a premium skylight to add to the overall ambiance.

Cargo capacity

PALISADE gives you the additional cargo room you need, to the tune of 86.7 cubic-feet with both 2nd- and 3rd-row seats folded down and 46.3 cubic-feet with just the 3rd-row seats folded down.

2nd-row captain’s chairs

The available captain's chairs make the already comfortable PALISADE even more so by giving 2nd-row passengers more space along with modern, upscale refinement. For bigger crowds, 2nd-row bench seating that accommodates up to 8 passengers comes standard.

2027 PALISADE with 3rd-row seating

The 3rd-row seating in PALISADE allows you to bring even more passengers along for the journey. With comfortable seating, plenty of leg room, and USB chargers, 3rd-row passengers can be just as relaxed as those in the front or 2nd-row.

Power-folding outside mirrors
