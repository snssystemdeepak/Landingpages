Intelligent Forward Collision Warning

What’s up ahead? Intelligent Forward Collision Warning watches up to two cars ahead, and when it detects sudden deceleration in front of you, it gives you a warning to slow down.*

Automatic Emergency Braking with Pedestrian Detection

Pathfinder’s Automatic Emergency Braking with Pedestrian Detection can warn you of hazards ahead and help brake to avoid or reduce frontal collisions, including stopping for pedestrians in the crosswalk.*

Rear Automatic Braking

Pathfinder’s standard Rear Automatic Braking monitors for large objects behind you and can automatically brake to help avoid or reduce rear collisions.*

Blind Spot Warning

Wait — was that a car over there? Pathfinder® with Blind Spot Warning helps keep an eye on the blind spot area and gives you a warning if it detects a vehicle hiding there.*

Rear Cross Traffic Alert

Fear no parking lot or driveway. Rear Cross Traffic Alert monitors behind your Pathfinder® and can warn you about cars approaching from either side as you back out.*

Lane Departure Warning

Hold it right there. Lane Departure Warning helps ensure you change lanes intentionally, alerting you with a gentle steering wheel buzz if you drift.*
