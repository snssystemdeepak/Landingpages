Get to Know the 2026 Nissan Murano® Chattanooga, TN, from Nissan of Chattanooga

Some crossovers are built mainly around practicality. It seats five passengers comfortably while offering the technology, climate features, and cargo flexibility needed for daily routines and longer trips. Nissan of Chattanooga offers three trim levels to explore: SV, SL, and Platinum.

Every configuration of the 2026 Nissan Murano® Chattanooga, TN, is powered by a 2.0-liter VC-Turbo® 4-cylinder engine producing 241 horsepower at 5,600 rpm and 260 lb-ft of torque at 4,000 rpm. A 9-speed automatic transmission handles gear changes, while standard all-wheel drive adds reassuring traction when roads become wet or winding. Fuel economy is rated at an estimated 21 city/27 highway/23 combined MPG.

Nissan equips the 2026 Nissan Murano® Chattanooga, TN, with a generous collection of standard driver-assistance technology. Intelligent Forward Collision Warning with Automatic Emergency Braking and Pedestrian Detection can help drivers respond to hazards ahead. An available HD Enhanced Intelligent Around View® Monitor provides a broader perspective when parking or navigating tight spaces.

The exterior gives the 2026 Nissan Murano® Chattanooga, TN, a confident road presence without looking overly aggressive. Standard 20-inch Gloss Black aluminum-alloy wheels complement the black grille, rear spoiler, and body-color bumpers. Automatic LED headlights, rear privacy glass, heated power mirrors, a power liftgate, and a capless fuel filler make the design practical as well as stylish. Available 21-inch machine-finished aluminum-alloy wheels create an even stronger stance, while panoramic first- and second-row moonroofs bring more natural light into the cabin. Power-folding mirrors, rain-detecting windshield wipers, and Ground Lighting with Logo are also available.

Inside, standard Prima-Tex® seating gives the five-passenger cabin a refined starting point. Heated front seats, an 8-way power driver’s seat, and a 6-way power front passenger seat help both front occupants settle in comfortably. Dual Zone Automatic Temperature Control with rear vents allows passengers to manage different climate preferences, while the 60/40 split-folding rear seat expands cargo flexibility.

Available quilted leather seats bring a richer finish to the 2026 Nissan Murano® Chattanooga, TN. Ventilated and massaging front seats make longer drives easier, while heated rear seats add comfort for passengers. Other available upgrades include a heated steering wheel, ambient lighting, an auto-dimming rearview mirror, and a power tilt/telescoping steering wheel.

Technology is centered around a standard 12.3-inch NissanConnect® infotainment system and a matching 12.3-inch reconfigurable instrument cluster. Wireless Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™, Bluetooth®, four USB ports, wireless charging, keyless entry, remote start, and Nissan Intelligent Key® with Push Button Ignition are all included. The MyNISSAN app and NissanConnect® Services add connected convenience. Available upgrades include Google™ built-in, Google Maps™ navigation, a 10-speaker Bose® Premium Sound System, ProPILOT Assist 2.1, myQ® Connected Garage, and a Dual Camera Drive Recorder.
