FAQs About the 2026 RAM 1500 Ventura, CA

1. Which engines are available for the 2026 RAM 1500?

The lineup offers a 3.6-liter Pentastar® V-6 eTorque engine, a 5.7-liter HEMI® V-8 eTorque engine, and standard-output or high-output 3.0-liter Hurricane Twin Turbo engines.

2. How much can the 2026 RAM 1500 Oxnard, CA, tow?

When properly equipped, the 2026 RAM 1500 can tow up to 11,610 pounds. Towing capacity varies based on the engine, drivetrain, cab, bed, axle ratio, and equipment selected.

3. Is the HEMI® V-8 available in the 2026 RAM 1500?

Yes. The 5.7-liter HEMI® V-8 eTorque engine returns for 2026 with 395 horsepower and 410 lb-ft of torque. It is available on select trim levels.

4. What does the RAMBox® Cargo Management System provide?

The available RAMBox® system adds lockable, drainable, and illuminated storage compartments along the sides of the truck bed, creating space for tools, equipment, or recreational gear.

5. Does the 2026 RAM 1500 Ventura, CA, offer hands-free driving?

An available Hands-Free Active Driving Assist System can help manage steering and speed on compatible roads, although drivers must remain attentive and ready to take control.
