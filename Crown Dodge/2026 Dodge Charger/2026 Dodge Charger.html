1. Is the 2026 Dodge Charger available with two or four doors?

Yes. Gasoline-powered R/T and Scat Pack models are available as two-door coupes or four-door sedans, giving drivers the choice between a classic muscle-car profile and easier rear-seat access.

The R/T produces up to 420 horsepower, while the Scat Pack delivers as much as 550 horsepower. The electric Daytona Scat Pack generates up to 670 horsepower.

3. Does the 2026 Dodge Charger have all-wheel drive?

Yes. All 2026 Dodge Charger trim levels include standard all-wheel drive. SIXPACK-powered versions also offer a rear-wheel-drive mode for a more traditional performance feel.

The R/T can travel from zero to 60 MPH in approximately 4.6 seconds, the Scat Pack does it in about 3.9 seconds, and the electric Daytona Scat Pack can reach 60 MPH in roughly 3.3 seconds.

Yes. The rear hatch is integrated into the Charger’s fastback-style body, creating a wide cargo opening. The rear seats can also fold flat to accommodate larger items.
