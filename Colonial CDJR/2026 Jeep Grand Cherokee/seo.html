The standard configuration features a reliable 3.6-liter V-6 engine delivering 293 horsepower. For increased efficiency and torque, an optional 2.0-liter Hurricane 4 Turbo engine produces 324 horsepower and 332 lb-ft of torque, capable of achieving up to 27 MPG on the highway. Both pair seamlessly with the Gen IV 8-Speed Automatic transmission.

The visual presence of the 2026 Jeep® Grand Cherokee Hudson, MA, is bolder than ever. The exterior benefits from a significant styling refresh, highlighting new front fascias, a redesigned grille, and striking newly designed LED headlamps. It rolls on standard 18-inch wheels, but Colonial Hudson CDJR customers can customize their ride with various finishes, including 18-Inch High-Gloss Black wheels, 18-Inch Polished with High-Gloss Black Pockets, or 18-Inch Machined Face with Black Noise Accents. Loading cargo is effortless with the available Hands-Free Power Liftgate.

The cabin is a technological marvel. Every 2026 Jeep® Grand Cherokee Hudson, MA, now includes a massive 12.3-inch Uconnect® 5 NAV touchscreen display perfectly paired with a newly standard 10.25-Inch Digital Cluster Display. Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™ compatibility are standard, alongside a convenient wireless charging pad and a Digital Rearview Mirror. For passengers, the Class-Exclusive Front Passenger Interactive Touchscreen Display provides dedicated entertainment and navigation assistance. You can also upgrade from the standard 6-speaker stereo to either a 9-speaker Alpine stereo or the breathtaking 19-speaker McIntosh® sound system. Families will also appreciate the available Interior FamCAM, rear-seat entertainment screens, and the 10-inch full-color head-up display.

Versatility defines the interior space of the 2026 Jeep® Grand Cherokee Hudson, MA. Depending on your needs, seating capacity ranges from five to seven passengers. Standard LATCH anchor systems, 70.8 cubic feet of cargo room, and front bucket seats provide a secure foundation. When properly configured, the cabin offers up to 84.6 cubic feet of cargo room. Luxury reaches new heights with available Nappa leather seats featuring red stitching, teak and satin chrome accents, and multicolor ambient LED lighting. Drivers and passengers can relax with optional heated, ventilated, and reclining seats, while memory seat presets and massage mode ensure fatigue-free travel. A power-folding third-row bench and second row captain chairs maximize seating flexibility.

Protecting your family is the top priority. The standard safety suite is comprehensive, featuring intersection collision assist, full-speed forward collision warnings with pedestrian and cyclist detection, automated emergency braking, and blind-spot monitoring with rear cross-path detection. Active lane management and lane-keeping assist keep you centered, while adaptive cruise control eases highway driving, aided by the Parkview® Back-up camera. For enhanced awareness, buyers can select the available 360-Degree Surround View Camera, Drowsy Driver Detection, Night Vision Camera, Parallel and Perpendicular Park Assist, and the innovative Hands-Free Active Driving Assistant.
