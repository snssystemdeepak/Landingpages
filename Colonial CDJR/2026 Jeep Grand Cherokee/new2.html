The Peace of Safety and Security - Elevated Confidence

The new 2026 Jeep® Grand Cherokee offers the most standard and available safety and security features in its class*, so that drivers looking to go one peak above standard can carefully monitor their surroundings and receive helpful input as they go.

Hands-Free Active Driving Assist

Comfort and convenience take the wheel with new available Hands-Free Active Driving Assist*. By combining sensor data and speed control, the system allows you to remove your hands from the wheel while keeping your eyes on the road. The system then monitors driver attentiveness, and issues visual and audible alerts if it notices you are distracted or fatigued.

Night Vision Camera

While sneaky nocturnal critters may not like it, the available Night Vision Camera uses infrared sensors to scan for pedestrians and animals up to 328 feet in front of the vehicle.

360º Surround View Camera

The available 360º Surround View Camera* offers an aerial view of the vehicle and its surroundings to help you navigate narrow trails and prepare for upcoming obstacles.

Drowsy Driver Detection

Available Drowsy Driver Detection* examines driver interaction and vehicle movement for patterns of drowsiness. If drowsiness is detected, the system can send you an audible and visual signal as a precaution.

Parallel And Perpendicular Park Assist

The available Parallel and Perpendicular Park Assist System* detects open parking spaces and steers the vehicle into them as it directs you to shift gears and operate the brakes and accelerator.

Active Driving Assist

The semiautonomous Active Driving Assist System* uses an array of cameras and sensors to make subtle adjustments to steering and acceleration so that you can remain a set distance from any vehicle directly ahead and stay centered in your lane.

Intersection Collision Assist

Intersection Collision Assist* uses sensors to detect oncoming vehicles at four-way intersections.

Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop And Go

Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop and Go* automatically adjusts your driving speed to maintain a preselected distance from the vehicle ahead.

Active Lane Management

The Active Lane Management System* measures your position within lane boundaries. If the system detects an unintentional lane drift, it can alert you and correct the vehicle's position within the lane. The system also provides steering input to help avoid collisions with vehicles in your blind spot zones.
