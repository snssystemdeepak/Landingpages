Safety and driver support

Feel ready for life’s twists and turns.*

Reverse with confidence

Cross Traffic Alert with auto brake supports the driver when reversing with a limited view. The system can detect vehicles crossing behind your car, and the auto brake can be applied if needed.

Help crossing oncoming traffic

Our safety assistance technology can help when you turn across oncoming traffic. If there’s a risk of collision, the car can automatically brake.

Avoid collisions

Day or night, our intelligent safety assistance technology can help you detect and avoid other vehicles, pedestrians, cyclists and large animals. It does this by warning the driver and automatically braking if the driver fails to react.

Stress less in busy traffic

Our blind spot information system (BLIS) provides active support when changing lanes. If there is a risk of collision with other vehicles in the adjacent lane, BLIS can subtly adjust steering, helping keep you, your car and your loved ones right where you mean to be.

Detects and corrects drifting

If you start drifting across the center line, a collision with an oncoming vehicle is always possible. Your car can detect this and correct the steering, helping you back to a safer position on the road.

Available 360° parking view

With four cameras providing a 360° view and side sensors increasing awareness of objects at the side of the car, this system can help you park confidently even in confined spaces.
