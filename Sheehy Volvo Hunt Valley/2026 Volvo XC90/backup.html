11.2-inch center display

Control navigation, entertainment and driver support functions from the free-standing 11.2-inch center display. Its anti-glare touchscreen means maps, media and more are displayed with crisp readability, even in full sun.

The display is designed to help keep your attention on the road. Its vertical orientation shows more of what's ahead when navigating.

A fixed menu on the bottom gives you quick access to the home screen, climate controls and car settings.

It'll also serve shortcuts to the most-used functions like drive modes, the available 360° camera, recent apps and Google™ Assistant. The display will only prompt relevant actions, whether you're parked, driving or on call.

12.3-inch driver display

Get driving information exactly how you want it on the 12.3-inch driver display. Choose from two views to suit your driving style.

Head-up display

Opt for the head-up display to keep your eyes on the road even more of the time. It projects essential information like speed, speed limits and navigation prompts directly onto the windshield so they appear to hover over the hood. There’s less need to look down or shift your gaze.

The display stays crisp and clear whether you're driving through a tunnel or after dark. You can also adjust the brightness and position to suit your preferences.

Two drive modes

Use the center display to toggle between two drive modes without returning to settings. Multi-zone climate controls

Front passengers can set their own temperature and fan settings through the center display. Floor air outlets and adjustable roof vents help keep rear passengers comfortable.

Our standard four-zone climate system allows front-row and second-row passengers to set individual temperatures. Rear-seat passengers manage settings from a touch panel at the back of the center console.

Floor air outlets and adjustable air vents in the door pillars help keep rear passengers comfortable.

Bring the cabin to a comfortable temperature and run an air purification cycle before you get in using the Volvo Cars app

Advanced air quality controls

The air quality system continuously filters and monitors incoming air and automatically shuts the cabin's intakes if harmful substances like ozone or exhaust gases are detected.

An air purifier is designed to remove up to 95 percent of PM2.5 particles, which can affect your respiratory health. It also eliminates up to 97 percent of airborne viruses and 99.9 percent of pollen.

Check cabin air quality on the center display and use it to run an air purification cycle. You can also refresh the cabin remotely using the Volvo Cars app.

Child seats and locks

Child seats can be installed in any of the three rear seats with ISOFIX anchorages in the outer positions.

Integrated child seats
When your little ones outgrow their child seats, they can transition to the available built-in booster cushion in the center of the second row, which supports a proper seatbelt fit and comfortable seating. Not available in the 6-seat configuration.

Power child lock
The standard power child lock on the rear doors and windows is controlled directly from the driver's seat.

Ride comfort

Enhanced sound insulation and strategically placed acoustic foam help block wind and road noise. For added peace and quiet, opt for laminated side windows. Of course, suspension design also impacts ride comfort.

The standard chassis with Frequency Selective Damping technology adapts to road conditions for a more controlled drive. The dampers were precisely tuned on demanding Catalonian rally stages, the rugged German Eifel mountains and Volvo’s own testing grounds in Sweden.

The optional active chassis with air suspension gives you an even smoother road feel by adjusting ride height and stiffness. It continuously monitors the car, road and driver, adapting 500 times per second for improved cornering and reduced pitch and roll.

Cabin-wide comfort

Practical storage

There’s storage for your things under the upholstered armrest of the center console. There's also a little storage space under the roll-top cover, plus cupholders for two beverages and a slim can.

Everyone has a cupholder within easy reach. In the 7-seat configurations, second-row passengers have cupholders in the fold-down armrest. In the 6-seater, cupholders are integrated into the armrest of the second-row seats. There are also cupholders in all third-row armrests.

Panoramic roof

The panoramic roof in the XC90 stretches from the windshield to behind the rear seats, flooding the cabin with natural light. The dark-tinted laminated glass helps reduce glare and noise inside the cabin. It also strengthens the glass for added security.

Press a button on the front-row ceiling console to open the roof and let fresh air flow inside. If you forget to close it after parking, do it remotely with your key fob.

Safety and driver support

Because we all have precious cargo.*

Reverse with confidence

Cross Traffic Alert with auto brake supports the driver when reversing with a limited view. The system can detect vehicles crossing behind your car, and the auto brake can be applied if needed.

Help crossing oncoming traffic

Our safety assistance technology can help when you turn across oncoming traffic. If there’s a risk of collision, the car can automatically brake.

Avoid collisions

Day or night, our intelligent safety assistance technology can help you detect and avoid other vehicles, pedestrians, cyclists and large animals. It does this by warning the driver and automatically braking if the driver fails to react.

Stress less in busy traffic

Our blind spot information system (BLIS) provides active support when changing lanes. If there is a risk of collision with other vehicles in the adjacent lane, BLIS can subtly adjust steering, helping keep you, your car and your loved ones where you mean to be.

Detects and corrects drifting

If you start drifting across the center line, a collision with an oncoming vehicle is always possible. Your car can detect this and correct the steering, helping you back to a safer position on the road.

Available 360° parking view with split screen

Slip in and out of tight parking spots with the help of an available 360° camera view. Front, rear and side cameras produce a high-resolution bird's-eye view you can see on your center display. On-screen graphics show the car's intended path to help you maneuver.

The split-screen function means you can also see a nearly 180° front or rear view at the same time.

Hitching a trailer? Activate the zoom function on your 180° view to get a closer look.
