1. What size infotainment screen comes inside the truck?

The 2026 Nissan Frontier® Dalton, GA, features a standard 8-inch touchscreen, but drivers can upgrade their digital workspace with an available massive 12.3-inch touchscreen for enhanced visibility and control.

Yes! This Nissan pickup features a standard Wi-Fi hotspot that includes a 3-GB or 90-day trial (whichever occurs first), allowing you to easily keep your tablet or laptop connected while working remotely.

Absolutely. Drivers who plan to take their truck on the trails can opt for the available 17-inch PRO aluminum-alloy wheels, which come wrapped in aggressive all-terrain tires for maximum grip.

Yes, you can keep your cabin completely free of cord clutter by adding the highly convenient optional wireless charger built right into the center console.
