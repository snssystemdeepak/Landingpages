Cargo Dimensions

Altima features ample trunk space, with up to 15.4 cu. ft. Fold the rear seats down, and expand the area to a massive 58.1 cu. ft. of cargo space. This allows for great possibilities, including space for luggage on road trips, supply runs for parties, or for that side table you’ve been eyeing.*

60/40-split Rear Seats

DRIVER ASSIST & SAFETY TECH

Tech that drives confidence

ProPILOT Assist

Available ProPILOT Assist helps you stay centered in the lane and helps keep a preset distance between you and the car ahead. It can even bring your Altima® to a complete stop and then back up to speed when traffic starts moving again.*

Intelligent Around View® MONITOR

The available Intelligent Around View® Monitor gives you a virtual composite 360° view, helping you park with ease.*

INTELLIGENT FORWARD COLLISION WARNING

Intelligent Forward Collision Warning watches two cars ahead, and when it detects sudden deceleration in front of you, it gives you a warning to slow down.*

Nissan Safety Shield® 360

Helping to watch out for you and those around you, Nissan is creating a brighter future by making Safety Shield® 360 standard on every Altima®.*

Automatic Emergency Braking with Pedestrian Detection

Altima® helps look out for what’s ahead and for people crossing in front of you. Automatic Emergency Braking with Pedestrian Detection monitors your speed and the distance between you and the car ahead, and it can let you know if you need to slow down. It can automatically engage the brakes to help avoid a frontal collision or lessen the severity of an impact. And when it detects a pedestrian in the crosswalk, it can stop you in your tracks.*

Rear Automatic Braking

Save your bumper. Altima® with Rear Automatic Braking watches out directly behind you for large stationary objects you might not see. If it detects something and you fail to stop, it can automatically engage the brakes to help avoid a rear collision or lessen the severity of an impact.*

Blind Spot Warning

Never miss a beat. Altima® with Blind Spot Warning keeps an eye on the blind spot area and gives you a heads-up if it detects a vehicle hiding there.*

Rear Cross Traffic Alert

Fear no parking lot. When you’re backing out of a space, Rear Cross Traffic Alert watches around the rear of your Altima® and can warn you about cars it detects creeping up on you from either side.*

Lane Departure Warning

Keep yourself centered. Lane Departure Warning helps make sure you change lanes only when you mean to. If the system detects that you’re drifting over, it’ll warn you.*

High Beam Assist

High Beam Assist helps you during late-night drives by turning down your high beams when it senses oncoming traffic.
