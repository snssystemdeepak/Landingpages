Powering the 2026 Nissan Altima® Baltimore, MD is a 2.5-liter 4-cylinder engine that produces 188 horsepower at 6,000 rpm and 180 lb-ft of torque at 3,600 rpm. Paired with the Xtronic CVT®, this Nissan sedan delivers smooth acceleration, and a refined driving feel that suits both city streets and highway travel. For those who want added traction, available all-wheel drive adjusts output slightly to 182 horsepower and 178 lb-ft of torque.

Inside the 2026 Nissan Altima® Baltimore, MD, the cabin is designed with everyday usability in mind. Seating for five passengers provides ample room, while the 60/40 split-folding rear bench seat adds flexibility for cargo when needed. Standard features include cloth seating, an 8-way power driver’s seat, and a 4-way manually adjustable front passenger seat, allowing drivers to find a comfortable position with ease. Additional touches such as welcome lighting and illuminated entry enhance the experience when entering the vehicle, while air conditioning and power windows support everyday comfort. Available upgrades introduce more refinement, including heated front seats, a heated steering wheel, and a dual-zone automatic temperature control system with rear seat vents. Drivers can also add a frameless auto-dimming rearview mirror with HomeLink® for added convenience and functionality.

The 2026 Nissan Altima® Baltimore, MD, offers a well-equipped technology suite that keeps drivers connected and informed. Standard features include an 8-inch NissanConnect® touchscreen, a 7-inch reconfigurable LCD instrument cluster, and a 6-speaker audio system. Smartphone integration comes standard with Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™, along with Bluetooth®, two USB ports, and Siri® Eyes Free/Google Assistant™ compatibility. Drivers can access additional convenience through the Remote Engine Start System, remote keyless entry, and Nissan Intelligent Key® with a Push Button Ignition. Available upgrades expand the experience with a larger 12.3-inch touchscreen, a Bose® premium sound system, a Wi-Fi hotspot, and MyNISSAN connectivity. Additional available features include wireless Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™, a wireless charger, and ProPILOT Assist for added driver support.

The exterior of the 2026 Nissan Altima® Baltimore, MD, presents a sleek and contemporary design. This Nissan sedan comes standard with 17-inch machine-finished aluminum-alloy wheels, Intelligent Auto LED headlights, and a black grille with chrome surround that adds a refined touch. LED front exterior accent lights enhance visibility and style, while body-color bumpers and power mirrors maintain a cohesive look. Upgrade for 19-inch aluminum-alloy wheels, a moonroof, and ground lighting with logo projection. Safety remains a priority in the 2026 Nissan Altima® Baltimore, MD, with a comprehensive set of standard driver-assistance features. These include Intelligent Forward Collision Warnings with Automatic Emergency Braking, Rear Cross Traffic Alerts with Rear Automatic Braking, and Pedestrian Detection. Additional systems such as Blind Spot Warnings, Lane Departure Warnings, and Driver Attention Alerts help support awareness in various driving conditions. Available features such as the Intelligent Around View® Monitor and adaptive cruise control provide added assistance for parking and highway driving.
