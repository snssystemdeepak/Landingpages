Meet the 2026 Ford Bronco® Richmond, VA, at Sheehy Ford

Drivers who want an SUV that feels bold, modular and trail-ready will find plenty to love in the 2026 Ford Bronco® Richmond, VA. Available now at Sheehy Ford, this 4x4 icon blends rugged capability with modern displays, smart connectivity and trim choices that let the Ford Bronco® fit different lifestyles.

Which Ford dealers have the 2026 Ford Bronco® in Richmond, VA, in stock?

Sheehy Ford does. The appeal of the 2026 Ford Bronco® Richmond, VA, begins with how it is engineered for confident traction and terrain flexibility. Standard power comes from a 2.3L EcoBoost® I-4 engine producing 300 horsepower, paired with a 7-speed manual transmission and standard 4x4. This setup gives this Ford SUV a hands-on driving feel while still delivering the kind of controlled strength SUV buyers want when roads turn rough. A Terrain Management System with 5 G.O.A.T. Modes® comes standard. For drivers who want additional output with automatic shifting, the available 2.7L EcoBoost® V6 generates 330 horsepower and pairs with a 10-speed automatic transmission. This configuration also includes HOSS 4.0 with FOX™ Live Valve 3.1 internal bypass semi-active dampers and expands the Terrain Management System to 7 G.O.A.T. Modes®, giving the 2026 Ford Bronco® Richmond, VA, even more control over challenging terrain. For maximum capability, the available 3.0L EcoBoost® V6 engine increases power to 418 horsepower, designed for those who want the most aggressive performance option in the lineup.

The exterior design of the 2026 Ford Bronco® Richmond, VA, is not only iconic, it is also functional. Standard features include a Carbonized Gray molded-in-color hard top (2-door), black door handles, a black MIC grille with white Bronco® lettering, and LED headlamps. The 2026 Ford Bronco® Richmond, VA, also includes two front and one rear tow hooks, plus a manual swing-gate with up to a 150-degree swing. The removable doors and top keep the vehicle customizable, while power heated mirrors with manual fold add daily comfort. Standard wheels are 16-inch bright polished silver-painted steel. Optional exterior equipment adds another layer of character. Available upgrades include dual tops with an Oxford White-painted hard top and black soft top, LED foglamps, LED approach lamps, and full underbody bash plates. Drivers can also add a heavy-duty modular front bumper with removable end caps and Rigid® LED fog and off-road lamps, plus Raptor®-style running boards and 17-inch black high gloss-painted alloy wheels.

The cabin of the 2026 Ford Bronco® Richmond, VA, is designed with functionality in mind, making it easy to transition between weekday commuting and weekend trail use. Cloth seating surfaces and 6-way manually adjustable front seats keep the entry models straightforward and durable. Available interior upgrades give the 2026 Ford Bronco® Richmond, VA, a more refined or more rugged feel, depending on preference. Drivers can add dual-zone electronic automatic temperature control for improved cabin comfort. Options such as heated front seats and a heated steering wheel support cold mornings, while leather seats bring a more premium edge.

Technology in the 2026 Ford Bronco® Richmond, VA, is centered around large displays and easy smartphone integration. Standard equipment includes a 12-inch LCD digital instrument cluster and a 12-inch LCD capacitive center display with swipe capability. SYNC® 4 comes standard, along with wireless Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™, making it simple to connect music, navigation and communication. This Ford SUV also includes Remote Keyless Access, USB ports and FordPass Connect™. Available upgrades boost both convenience and entertainment. Drivers can add remote start, Intelligent Access, navigation and enhanced voice recognition.

Safety features support the adventurous nature of the 2026 Ford Bronco® Richmond, VA, by helping drivers stay aware in unpredictable conditions. Standard systems include Pre-Collision Assist with Automatic Emergency Braking, Hill Start Assist, Post Collision Braking, a rear view camera and Auto High-Beam headlamps. Drivers who want more advanced support can opt for available features such as the Lane-Keeping System, BLIS® with Cross-Traffic Alerts, Adaptive Cruise Control, Evasive Steering Assist and a 360-degree camera.
