G.O.A.T. Modes®

Ford Bronco® G.O.A.T. Modes® (Goes Over Any Type of Terrain) can take on roads, trails or two-tracks in nearly any condition.

Normal

Engineered for everyday driving, this default mode is the perfect balance of practicality and performance. Standard on all models.

Eco

Save your fuel for the trail. This mode alters engine tuning to focus on efficiency. Standard on all models.

Sand

For off-road driving on soft, dry sand or deep snow. This mode may help get your vehicle unstuck from deep snow or sand. Standard on all models.

Mud/Ruts

For off-road driving, this mode enhances vehicle performance to traverse muddy, rutted or uneven terrains. Standard on Badlands®, Stroppe® Edition and Raptor® models.

Baja

Love off-roading at high speed? This mode is for you. Optimized throttle control improves responsiveness and torque delivery. Standard on Badlands®, Stroppe® Edition and Raptor® models.

Slippery

This mode is perfect for when road conditions... aren’t. Lower throttle response, optimized shifting and more restrictive traction and stability controls give you tighter control on snow, ice, or any wet or slick terrain. Standard on all models.

Rock Crawl

Take your adventure up a few notches. This mode engages the four-wheel drive lock and rear differential lock. At slow speeds, the front trail camera turns on to give you a better view of oncoming obstacles. Standard on Badlands®, Stroppe® Edition and Raptor® models.

Sport

Increased accelerator response, improved performance and handling, and a tighter steering feel come together to make highway driving as fun as off-roading... almost. Standard on Base, Big Bend®, Heritage Edition and Outer Banks® models.

Trail Toolbox

Trail Turn Assist

You’ll run circles around others... as well as trees, rocks and almost anything else. Trail Turn Assist applies the brake to the inside rear wheel during low-speed, high steering-angle maneuvers to deliver a tighter turning radius.*

Ford Trail Control™

For those times you just want to take in the scenery. Just push the button, and this off-road cruise control for low-speed driving lets you enjoy your outdoor adventure even more.*

Trail 1-Pedal Drive

This terrain-taming feature lets the accelerator pedal take total control. Lift your foot off and the brakes kick in automatically. Press down, and the brakes release. It also configures drive mode settings as needed, making off-roading easier.*

AdvanceTrac® w/Roll Stability Control™ (RSC®)

This electronic stability control system helps to improve vehicle control on all driving surfaces, including snow, ice, gravel, rain-covered roads and dry pavement, while the AdvanceTrac® Roll Stability Control (RSC) offers increased rollover protection.

The HOSS System

All of the available High-Performance Off-Road Stability Suspension (HOSS) Systems deliver the kind of confident control and hardy handling Bronco® drivers deserve.

Bronco® Raptor® HOSS 4.0 SUSPENSION SYSTEM

Only available on Raptor® models. Standard features include:

•Live Valve technology with position-sensitive damping
•3.1 internal bypass semi-active dampers
•Integrated front reservoirs
•Remote reservoirs in rear

HOSS 3.0 Suspension System

The component upgrades of a true trail champion. A heavy-duty steering rack for 40% gain in maximum load. A rear stabilizer bar and more. Available on Badlands® model. Standard on Stroppe® Edition model. Features in addition to the included Sasquatch® Package:

•Live Valve 3.1 internal bypass semi-active dampers
•Front steel bash plates
•Ford Performance severe-duty steering rack and tie rod ends

HOSS 2.0 Suspension System

When you’re ready to dial up your adventure, this system is ready to deliver. Standard on Badlands® and Heritage Edition models, and on any model equipped with the Sasquatch® Package.

•Passive damping system
•Twin forged alloy A-arms with long-travel HOSS-tuned Bilstein Position-Sensitive Dampers with end-stop control
•End cap control valves
•Independent front suspension

HOSS 1.0 Suspension System

High-strength, specially tuned hardware calibrated specifically for higher-speed trail running. Standard on Outer Banks® and Base models.

•Twin forged alloy A-arms with long-travel coil-over springs

•220 mm solid rear axle with long-travel, variable-rate coil-over springs
